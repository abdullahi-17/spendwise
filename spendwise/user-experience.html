01 Core flow

Browser demo flow, step by step.

The implemented browser demo starts with a sample item. Answer three questions, optionally pause or write a reflection, then choose a simulated outcome. Back preserves answers, and Exit is available throughout. The mobile concept below includes future capabilities and is not the implemented architecture.

01
Start a check inStart manually with the sample purchase. No shopping-app detection is connected.
02
Quiet scanOptional local camera mirror. No emotion analysis or recording.
03
Three questionsFeeling, planned or not, could it wait 20 minutes. Order varies.
04
Grounding pauseTen seconds, only if the answer was "I need it now".
05
Own wordsOne line on why this, why now. Skippable.
06
InsightOne sentence from Nudge. Never a score, never a verdict.
07
Your callConsider later, dismiss the sample item, or continue shopping. All three options have equal visual emphasis.
08
Decision summarySee the simulated outcome or start another reflection. No history, purchase, or reminder is created.
The reflection can be skipped or closed at any time. SpendWise does not block shopping or require completion before a purchase. The app supports a decision without taking control of it.

03 Design system

Rules that make "no judgement" visible.

Principles

Witness, never judge

No warning colours or verdicts on past purchases.

Every outcome is a win

Buying, pausing, and removing are presented without judgement. The browser demo calculates no score and gives all three decisions equal emphasis.

Quiet by default

The notification design avoids revealing financial or emotional details in notifications.

On device first

Users report how they feel directly. Any future camera feature would be optional and evaluated separately.

Colour

The visual direction uses navy for hierarchy, blue for primary actions, and pale surfaces to separate content. Colour supports the hierarchy; labels and focus indicators also identify actions and states. The portfolio pages illustrate this direction. The production interface would still need usability and contrast verification.

Nudge is the named coach. It asks more than it tells, opens with something specific and observed, and never uses clinical or moralising language.

04 Psychology in the interface

Each mechanic has a reason, and a risk.

MechanicPrincipleRisk to watch
Question order variesDesign hypothesis: varied order may encourage attentionHarder to build consistent pattern data
10 second pause on "I need it now"Friction scales with urgencyUsers abandon the flow instead of waiting
Three equally styled outcomesKeep each decision easy to findWording and order may still influence choices; evaluate with users
Optional line in the user's own wordsDesign hypothesis: writing may support reflectionMay read as friction. Untested
Neutral decision summary without scoringDescribe the selected outcomeUsers may mistake a simulated outcome for a real action; disclose the boundary
These are design hypotheses and observed interface choices, not demonstrated behavioural effects. Test whether the questions and optional pause feel useful. Keep all decisions visible and treat reported pressure or discomfort as a reason to revise the flow.
